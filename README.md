## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is a major business challenge that can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect workforce stability.

This project analyzes employee data to understand **why employees leave the organization, which employee groups are at higher risk, and what actions HR teams can take to improve employee retention.**

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow:

**Python → SQL → Power BI → Business Insights → Recommendations**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The organization wants to understand employee attrition patterns and identify the factors associated with higher employee turnover.

The analysis focuses on:

- Which departments have higher attrition?
- Which job roles are at higher risk?
- Which age groups show higher employee turnover?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- How do overtime and other employee characteristics relate to attrition?
- Which employee segments should HR prioritize for retention?
