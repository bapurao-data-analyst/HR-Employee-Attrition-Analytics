# HR Employee Attrition Analytics

An end-to-end HR analytics project focused on understanding employee attrition patterns, identifying high-risk employee segments, and generating actionable business insights for employee retention.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations because high employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect workforce stability.

This project analyzes employee data to understand the major patterns associated with employee attrition and identifies high-risk departments, job roles, and employee segments.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow:

**Python → MySQL → Power BI → Business Insights & Recommendations**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The organization wants to understand:

- How many employees are leaving the organization?
- Which departments and job roles have higher attrition?
- Which employee groups are at higher risk of leaving?
- How do factors such as age, gender, overtime, business travel, and job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- What actions can HR take to improve employee retention?

---

## 💡 Project Objective

The main objective is to analyze employee attrition data and convert the analysis into meaningful business insights that can support HR decision-making and employee retention strategies.
